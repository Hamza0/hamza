If Google has suspended or disabled your Business Profile, first check the reason for the restriction. A missing listing can affect phone calls, directions and customer enquiries, particularly for a small business that depends on local search.

I would want to understand what Google is reporting before making changes to the profile or preparing an appeal.

## Is your profile suspended or just not showing?

A business may rank lower in Google Maps without being suspended. Sign in to the account that manages the listing and check for a restriction notice.

Google's [suspended profile guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4569145) explains its appeal process. The decision may relate to the profile itself or to a restriction on the managing account.

## Compare your listing with your real business

For example, suppose a home-based cleaning company travels to customers and does not receive visitors at its own address. Its listing should reflect how the business operates, not try to look like a storefront.

I would compare the business name, service area and address with Google's [business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177). Accurate business information matters more than adding extra search terms to the name.

## Prepare before opening an appeal

Gather suitable evidence, such as a business registration or a relevant utility bill, where applicable. Check that the details match the business information you are asking Google to review.

Use Google's official appeal tool and follow the instructions it shows you, including any evidence submission deadline. Do not create another listing for the same business as a workaround.

Only Google decides whether to restore a restricted profile. I would never promise an approval or a particular ranking.

## Let me understand what changed

You can share your business name, public listing link and the exact restriction message. If the problem started after an address change, tell me that too.

Please keep registration documents, passwords and verification codes out of the enquiry. If sensitive evidence is needed for an appeal, submit it through Google's own process.
