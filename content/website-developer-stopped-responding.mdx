If your web developer stops responding, another developer may still be able to help with your website. The first thing I would check is which accounts your business controls. You do not need to rebuild just because the person who built the site is unavailable.

## Find the accounts your business owns

A website can involve several separate accounts. I would start by checking whether you can sign in to these:

- Your domain, where you renew your website address.
- Your hosting account, where the website runs.
- Your website editor, such as WordPress or Shopify.
- Your business email, payment account and other connected tools, where relevant.

Paying the website invoice is not the same as controlling all of those accounts. Find the original invoices and account emails. Do not send passwords or verification codes through a contact form.

## What happens if you cannot access the domain?

Suppose your business website is running, but you cannot change the address or renew it because your former developer controls the account. I would identify the domain provider and help you understand its ownership and recovery process.

[ICANN's domain guidance](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/faqs-f0-2012-02-25-en) explains why a domain registered by another person can require help from the registrar. For a Canadian .ca domain, check the rules and process of your .ca registrar. Recovery depends on the account details and proof of ownership.

Google has its own [Business Profile ownership request process](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4566671?hl=en) if you are authorized to manage the business but somebody else controls the listing.

## Keep the business running while you recover access

If your site and email work today, avoid cancelling accounts or moving the domain before you know what each one does. A migration can interrupt your [business email](/blog/moving-website-keep-email-working) if the relevant settings or subscription are lost.

Once the access picture is clear, I can assess whether you need a small update, a handover or a larger recovery. Tell me what you know. It is fine if you do not know the technical names of the accounts.
