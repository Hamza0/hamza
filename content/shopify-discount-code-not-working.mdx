If your Shopify discount code is not working, check whether the code is active and whether the customer's cart qualifies. A code can be valid and still not apply to a particular order.

If you have advertised a Black Friday, Cyber Monday or other promotion, I understand how frustrating it can be to discover this after customers have started shopping.

## Start with one cart that should qualify

Open the discount in your Shopify admin. Compare its rules with the offer you advertised.

- Is the discount active at the current time in your store's time zone?
- Are the exact products or variants in the customer's cart eligible?
- Does the order meet the minimum purchase requirement?
- Has the customer or store reached a usage limit?

Shopify explains these options in its [discount code guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-methods/discount-codes).

## What if the code works on one product but not another?

Suppose you advertised 20% off selected products. A customer adds a different product to their cart and enters the code. The discount may correctly exclude that item, even if your banner sounds like a storewide offer.

I would test an eligible product, an excluded product and a mixed cart. That tells us whether the discount rules or the promotion wording need attention.

## Check automatic discounts too

If your store already applies a sale or free-shipping offer automatically, a second code may not be allowed with it. Check the combination settings on both discounts against [Shopify's combination rules](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations).

I would avoid changing every promotion at once while your store is taking orders. First establish which combination you intended to offer.

## Tell me what your customers are seeing

If you share your store link, the public discount code and an example of a cart that should qualify, I can understand the problem before suggesting changes. Please don't send private customer or payment details.

If the discount applies but the payment itself fails, that is a different issue. See my note on [Shopify checkout payments](/blog/shopify-checkout-payment-not-working).
