Google's Search Console can show how often your website appears in its AI answers. Use that information alongside website visits and the enquiries you receive.

## Find the right report

Open the Generative AI performance report in Search Console. Google [introduced it in June 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) and states that worldwide rollout followed on August 31.

The report counts impressions: occasions when links to your site appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. You can examine them by page, country, device and date. It does not provide a dedicated total of AI clicks or enquiries.

If the report is missing, Google's [documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139) says there may be too few impressions. The site's setting for inclusion in AI features is another check. A missing report alone does not explain why a page is absent.

## Compare similar periods

Compare equal periods, such as four complete weeks against the previous four. Keep the page, country and device filters the same.

Record changes to the website and consider seasonal differences. If several things changed together, a rising graph cannot tell you which caused the increase.

The newest data can still change while Google finishes collecting it. Review individual pages as well as the total before deciding what a change means.

## Keep appearances and enquiries separate

| What you want to know | Where to check |
| --- | --- |
| Did links appear in AI answers? | Generative AI impressions |
| Did people visit? | Search reports and website analytics |
| Did visitors contact you? | Confirmed form submissions or contact records |
| Were those enquiries useful? | The requests and follow-up conversations |

These records do not automatically connect a particular AI appearance to a particular enquiry. More impressions alone do not prove that the business gained customers.

## Improve the page people reach

If visitors struggle to contact you, check the form and its instructions. If customers keep asking something the page should explain, improve that section.

The [guide to clearer search content](/blog/seo-and-aeo-start-with-clear-answers) covers those basics. Record what you changed so later comparisons have useful context.
