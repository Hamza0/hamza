Give your customers clear answers about what you do and how you can help. Then make those pages accessible to search engines and support your claims.

## Choose a question worth answering

SEO means improving a website for search. AEO describes helping content work in search experiences that provide answers, including AI answers.

Google's [current guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) keeps established SEO practices at the centre of this work. It emphasises useful, distinctive information and advises against unnecessary AI text files such as llms.txt.

I start with questions your customers ask and the searches bringing people to your site. When several questions belong together, one useful page can answer them without a new article for every wording.

## Show how you know

Help a reader judge your claims. If a system became faster, explain the measurement. If you offer to solve a problem, describe what your service includes.

My [business systems case study](/projects/production-operations-platform) explains its response-time measurements and the test data used. That context helps someone judge whether the result is relevant to them.

Link to reliable sources for technical advice. Google's [helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) also encourages clear sourcing and information that serves the intended reader.

## Make the page easy to understand

Use headings your customers recognise, short paragraphs and links to useful details. Keep essential information in readable text. Structured data, which describes the page for search engines, should match what your visitors can see.

For Google's AI features, a page must be indexed and eligible to appear with a search snippet. The site's Search Console setting must also allow inclusion in those features. Eligibility does not guarantee an appearance.

## Check what happens after publishing

I would review both the searches bringing people to your page and the enquiries you receive. If customers keep asking something the page should explain, that section probably needs more attention.

[Check Google AI appearances in Search Console](/blog/how-to-measure-google-ai-search-visibility), then review visits and enquiries separately. Update facts when they change and keep claims within what the results actually show.
