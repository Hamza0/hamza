Customers can browse your Shopify store and add products to their carts, but they cannot finish paying. Before changing the theme or installing another app, I would establish which stage is failing and how many customers it affects.

An abandoned checkout by itself is not proof that payments are broken. Some shoppers simply leave. A repeatable error or failed payment event is more useful.

## Find the payment attempt

In Shopify admin, look at an affected order or abandoned checkout and its timeline. Note the exact payment message and approximate time without sharing customer card details. [Shopify's payment troubleshooting guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/troubleshooting-checkout-payments) explains where failed attempts appear.

If just one card is declined, the customer's bank may be involved. If all payment methods suddenly fail, check [Shopify Status](https://www.shopifystatus.com/) and the payment provider's status. A restriction on a payment account may also need to be addressed directly with the provider.

## Do not confuse checkout problems with theme problems

Imagine a store owner has changed its product page before a promotion. Customers now reach the shipping stage but cannot choose delivery. The issue could be the product, destination or shipping setup rather than a payment failure.

I would reproduce the actual path from product page to checkout and identify the point where it stops. If a customer says they have already been charged, confirm the transaction status with your payment provider before asking them to pay again.

## Be especially careful during a sale

Use an isolated test environment where possible. Turning on [Shopify payment test mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/test-orders/payments-test-mode) on an active store can prevent customers from paying, so don't enable it casually during a promotion.
