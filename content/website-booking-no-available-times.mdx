Your calendar has free time, but customers cannot book an appointment through your website. I would first check whether the booking tool has available appointments and whether your website is showing that same information.

It helps to try the booking yourself as a customer rather than relying on what you see when signed in.

## Why a free calendar can still show no appointments

Imagine a clinic that closes at 5 p.m. A customer wants a 60-minute appointment at 4:30 p.m. Even though the calendar looks free, that appointment would finish after closing time.

Other booking rules may prevent the time from appearing too. The minimum notice, time zone, breaks between appointments and staff availability can all matter.

If you use Calendly, its [availability troubleshooting guide](https://calendly.com/help/how-to-troubleshoot-unavailable-times-that-should-be-available) describes how to inspect a particular missing time and see the relevant restriction.

## Compare the booking tool with the website

Open the booking provider's direct public link, if one exists, and compare it with the booking section embedded on your website.

If the direct link works but the embedded version does not, I would investigate the page integration. If both show no appointments, I would start with the booking provider and its calendar or scheduling rules.

For a custom booking app, I would check the information the app receives about available times and whether its server applies the intended business rules.

## Make a test booking

Use a test appointment that will not charge a customer or interfere with a real booking. Confirm that the customer sees a success message, the appointment appears in your calendar and the confirmation arrives.

If a customer can choose a time but cannot finish, that is worth investigating separately from missing availability.

Tell me which booking page is causing trouble and one example of a time that should have been available. That gives me somewhere specific to start, instead of changing your whole website.
