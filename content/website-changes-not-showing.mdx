Your website may be showing a saved copy, or the change may not be published on the page visitors use. Check the live page before changing settings.

## Open the page your visitors see

I start with the exact public web address, while logged out. Confirm that you completed the publishing step. A saved draft or an editor preview may show changes that visitors cannot see yet.

If you have a separate test website, check that you edited the live version. For a homepage change, confirm that the page you edited is the one your website uses as its homepage.

WordPress.com's [editor troubleshooting guide](https://wordpress.com/support/site-editor/troubleshoot-editor-issues/) also explains why the editing view can differ from the published page.

## Compare where the old version appears

A cache is a saved copy that helps pages load faster. Your browser, website host or a delivery network can each keep one. A delivery network stores copies of website content in different locations.

Try a private browser window and another device. This helps narrow the problem, but a private window does not bypass every saved copy held by your website host.

Suppose you publish new Saturday hours. Your phone still shows the old hours, while another device shows the update. I would check the phone’s saved copy before changing the page again.

WordPress's [guide to changes not appearing](https://wordpress.org/documentation/article/faq-i-make-changes-and-nothing-happens/) covers these different caching layers.

## Refresh the relevant copy

Once you confirm the correct page is published, follow your host's instructions to refresh its saved copy. Keep caching enabled. Where supported, refresh the affected page instead of clearing unrelated content. Cloudflare documents [refreshing cached content by web address](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cache/how-to/purge-cache/purge-by-single-file/).

Reopen the public address and check the actual change. Keep the address, publication time and affected devices together if the problem continues.

If your live page is correct but the search result is old, that needs a [separate Google search check](/blog/why-google-shows-old-website-text).
