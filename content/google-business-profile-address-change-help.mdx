A Google Business Profile address change may be waiting for review or involve verification. The exact message helps identify what needs attention before another change is made.

I helped a client resolve an address-change issue while they were losing business. Their identity and the details of the fix remain private. Here is the context that helps when reviewing a similar problem.

## What does the edit status mean?

Google's [edit-status guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038311?hl=en) describes three outcomes:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted | The updated information appears on the profile. |
| Pending | Google is still reviewing the change. |
| Not approved | Google has not accepted the change. |

Google says reviews usually take up to ten minutes, but can sometimes take up to thirty days. That is its review window, not a promised resolution time for your business.

## Is verification a different process?

Yes. An edit review concerns an information change. [Business verification](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242?hl=en) establishes control of the profile. Google decides which verification methods are available.

If one screen shows a pending address and another asks for verification, record both messages and where they appear. Neither message alone explains the profile's full history.

## Why does the business context matter?

Is the business moving, or is its existing address wrong? Do customers visit the location, or does the business travel to them? Google's [business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) distinguish address and service-area information.

The practical concern is what customers see when they try to find you. An unclear location can mean another call or map search before a visit.

My client's issue is one resolved case. Another profile needs its own assessment of the messages, business details and earlier changes.
