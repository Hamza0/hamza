If your new business address isn't showing on Google, start with the exact message. I use that context to understand whether the issue involves review, verification or something else.

I helped a client resolve an address-change issue while they were losing business. Their identity and the details of the fix remain private, but I can explain what helps when reviewing a similar problem.

## What does your edit status mean?

Google's [edit-status guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038311?hl=en) describes three outcomes:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted | Your updated information appears on the profile. |
| Pending | Google is still reviewing your change. |
| Not approved | Google has not accepted the change. |

Google says reviews usually take up to ten minutes, but can sometimes take up to thirty days. That describes its review window; it does not promise when your particular issue will be resolved.

## Are you also being asked to verify?

An edit review concerns an information change. [Business verification](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242?hl=en) establishes control of the profile. Google decides which verification methods you can use.

If you see a pending address on one screen and a verification request on another, note both messages and where they appear. I would want that full picture before drawing a conclusion.

## What do your customers need to see?

Are you moving, or correcting an existing address? Do customers visit you, or do you travel to them? Google's [business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) distinguish address and service-area information.

I want to understand how the listing affects someone trying to find your business. An unclear location can mean another phone call or map search before a visit.

Your profile needs its own assessment. The fact that I resolved one client's issue does not establish the cause of yours.
